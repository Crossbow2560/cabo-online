Cabo – the online card game

Cabo is a memory card game for 2 to 8 players where the lowest hand wins. This is a free, real-time multiplayer version you play in the browser on your phone, tablet or computer. No sign-up and no download.

How to play Cabo

Everyone is dealt four face-down cards and peeks at the two nearest ones. On your turn, draw a card and either swap it into your grid or discard it. Discarding certain cards uses their ability: a 7 or 8 lets you peek at one of your own cards, a 9 or 10 lets you spy on an opponent's card, a Jack or Queen makes a blind swap, and a black King lets you look at two cards and swap them.

Whenever a card is discarded, anyone can snap a card of the same rank from their hand, but a wrong snap costs a penalty card. When you think your hand is the lowest, call CABO: everyone else gets one last turn, then the cards are revealed and the lowest total wins the round.

Play with friends

Pick a nickname, create a room and share the invite link. Friends join straight from the link, and running scores are kept across rounds.
